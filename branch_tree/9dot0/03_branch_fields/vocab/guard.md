# vocab.branch guard

## no blind scan

Do not extract vocabulary from every file first.

README.md vocab is the baseline.

## no context overload

Do not load the whole Repo.SeungeFlow or Repo.9Dot0 into context.window.

Use local Linux, SQLite/PostgreSQL, reports, and Raw URLs.

## no word-list collapse

vocab.branch is not a flat word list.

A word is a trigger, not merely a keyword.

## no uncontrolled expansion

Etymology and origin may later branch into history.

Do not force etymology/history into pass1 vocab skeleton.

## no source merge

Raw URL, branch, commit hash, path, and source status must remain distinct.

## no final judgment

vocab.branch creates reader infrastructure.

It does not finalize interpretation.
