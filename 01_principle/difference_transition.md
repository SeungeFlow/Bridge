# Difference / Transition

Difference is not only separation.

Difference has two directions:

1. Distinction
2. Direction change

## Distinction

"Different" means that two states are separated.

Example:

- existence / non-existence
- observer / observed target
- inside / outside
- 0 / 1

## Direction Change

"틀리다" in this structure does not mean merely wrong.

It means that the direction of an existing flow has turned.

Difference can therefore mean:

- distinction
- transition
- direction change
- distance gap
- vector-position shift

## Loop vs Transition

Loop:

internal endpoint -> internal starting point

Transition:

internal endpoint -> external starting point

dot is the critical-between point that separates loop from transition.

## Water-Flow Principle

A strong current cannot be changed by pushing the water directly.

The direction of the current changes when the field, boundary, or channel is changed.

Trigger:
  opens a new operation field.

Anchoring:
  fixes the new direction.
